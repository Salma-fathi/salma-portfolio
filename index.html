01 Financial Analytics

Stock Market Intelligence

Stock Market Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end financial analytics project combining market data, technical indicators, predictive modeling, and portfolio simulation to investigate risk-adjusted investment decisions.

Question How do risk and return change across a technology-stock portfolio?
Analysis Technical indicators + time-series validation + simulation
Output Interactive analytical dashboard
Context

Compare five major technology stocks while avoiding simplistic return-only comparisons.

Approach

Built technical indicators including RSI, MACD and Bollinger Bands, followed by Gradient Boosting classification with time-aware validation and Monte Carlo portfolio simulation.

Finding

The simulated portfolio analysis produced a highest observed risk-adjusted configuration with a Sharpe Ratio of approximately 1.225 within the tested scenario.

Portfolio Finding

Portfolio construction should be evaluated through both expected return and risk-adjusted performance rather than looking at individual stock returns alone.

02 Fraud & Risk

Transaction Analytics

Financial Transaction & Fraud Analytics

A structured transaction analytics project designed to investigate fraud patterns, customer behavior, and the role of region and transaction channel in risk concentration.

Question Where and when does transaction risk concentrate?
Analysis Star schema + RFM + fraud pattern analysis
Output Analytical model + BI dashboard concept
Context

Financial transaction data can contain behavioral and operational signals that are difficult to interpret from raw transaction tables alone.

Approach

Designed a star-schema structure, transformed transaction data, performed customer RFM analysis, and investigated fraud concentration across channels and regions.

Finding

The analysis highlighted differences in risk concentration across customer segments, regions, and transaction channels, creating a basis for targeted monitoring.

Portfolio Finding

Fraud analysis becomes more actionable when transaction-level signals are connected with customer behavior and operational dimensions.

03 Customer Analytics

Olist Customer Intelligence

Olist Customer Intelligence: RFM & Retention

A customer analytics case study using PostgreSQL and Power BI to understand repeat purchasing, customer value, retention, and geographic patterns in Brazilian e-commerce data.

Question What does customer retention look like, and which segments deserve attention?
Analysis RFM segmentation + cohorts + geography
Output PostgreSQL model + Power BI analysis
Context

A large e-commerce dataset provides customer, order, product, payment, and geographic information but requires careful modeling before meaningful retention analysis.

Approach

Built SQL-based RFM scoring and customer segments, investigated repeat purchasing and cohort behavior, and created Power BI views for geographic and segment analysis.

Finding

The analyzed sample showed a repeat-purchase rate of approximately 3.12%, indicating a substantial retention challenge within the dataset.

Portfolio Finding

A low repeat-purchase rate shifts the business question from simply acquiring more customers toward understanding early customer experience and re-engagement opportunities.

04 Healthcare Analytics

Healthcare Readmission

Healthcare Analytics: Deep SQL Insights

A healthcare analytics project focused on patient readmission patterns, medication burden, admission characteristics, and risk stratification using advanced SQL.

Question Which patient and encounter characteristics are associated with readmission?
Analysis CTEs + window functions + quintile analysis
Output Analytical SQL model + risk insights
Context

Hospital readmissions can be investigated by connecting patient characteristics with encounter-level behavior and treatment-related variables.

Approach

Structured the dataset into analytical dimensions and facts, then used CTEs, window functions, and quintile analysis to investigate readmission-related patterns.

Finding

The analysis identified differences in readmission patterns across medication-load groups and encounter characteristics, supporting a more structured approach to risk investigation.

Portfolio Finding

Readmission analysis is more useful when patient-level characteristics are combined with encounter context instead of relying on a single risk variable.
