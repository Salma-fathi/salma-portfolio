How I Work

Every project follows a structured methodology designed to pass both recruiter scanning and technical screening.

Data Cleaning

  • Clear before/after snapshots of messy source data
  • Document deduplication, null handling, and structural modeling
  • Explain how cleaning changed downstream outcomes

STAR Framework

  • SSituation: The business blocker or risk
  • TTask: Exactly what you owned
  • AAction: SQL joins, Python transforms, validation
  • RResult: Measurable revenue or operational impact

Technical Screening

  • SQL window functions, CTE optimization, dimensional modeling
  • Star-schema thinking for warehouse-style analytics
  • One line reserved for the exact business metric improved
