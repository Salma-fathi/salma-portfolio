Project 01 / 03

E-Commerce Customer Intelligence

Python SQL Power BI XGBoost RFM Model Plotly

A major e-commerce client was losing high-value customers without knowing why. I built an end-to-end analytics pipeline — starting with RFM segmentation and CLV modeling across 1.2M+ transaction records, then training a churn prediction model (91.4% accuracy) to flag at-risk customers before they churned. The final Power BI dashboard gave the retention team a weekly action list, ranked by predicted revenue impact.

Business Impact
+12% customer retention rate within 90 days — translating to ~$1.4M in recovered recurring revenue for the client's top segment.

Project 02 / 03

Portfolio Risk & Allocation Analyzer

Python Pandas SQL Plotly Time Series SciPy

Portfolio managers across three investment funds were using incompatible spreadsheets — producing conflicting Sharpe ratios and delaying weekly allocation reviews by hours. I replaced this with a reproducible Python-based risk metric library: automated time-series ingestion, standardised VaR and drawdown calculations, and a Plotly dashboard that let managers compare allocations side-by-side in real time.

Business Impact
+18% decision confidence score (post-implementation survey) — weekly allocation review time cut from 4.5 hrs to under 45 min per fund manager.

Project 03 / 03

Healthcare Readmission Predictor

scikit-learn Python SQL Jupyter Feature Eng. GBM

A regional hospital system had no way to predict which patients were likely to return within 30 days of discharge — leading to costly readmissions and missed intervention windows. I engineered patient-level features from clinical and administrative data, trained a gradient-boosted classifier (AUC 0.971), and built a clinical dashboard that generates a daily priority queue so care coordinators can target post-discharge calls to the highest-risk patients first.

Business Impact
94.9% classification accuracy (AUC 0.971) — clinical team coverage of high-risk patients increased from 41% to 78%, reducing 30-day readmission rate by an estimated 19%.
