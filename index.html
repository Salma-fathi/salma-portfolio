How I Work

Every project follows a structured methodology designed to pass both recruiter scanning and technical screening.

01

Data Cleaning

  • Clear before/after snapshots of messy source data
  • Document deduplication, null handling, and structural modeling
  • Explain how cleaning changed downstream outcomes
02

STAR Framework

  • SSituation: The business blocker or risk
  • TTask: Exactly what you owned
  • AAction: SQL joins, Python transforms, validation
  • RResult: Measurable revenue or operational impact
03

Technical Screening

  • SQL window functions, CTE optimization, dimensional modeling
  • Star-schema thinking for warehouse-style analytics
  • One line reserved for the exact business metric improved

03 / 04

Olist Customer Intelligence: RFM & Retention Analytics

End-to-end PostgreSQL + Power BI case study on ~96K Brazilian e-commerce customers — RFM segmentation, cohort retention, and geographic revenue analysis surfaced in an executive dashboard.

SituationLeadership needed a clear view of which customers drive revenue and where retention was leaking, but the existing dashboard had geocoding errors and an unreadable RFM matrix.
TaskBuild the RFM scoring logic in PostgreSQL, then diagnose and fix the Power BI report so segmentation and geography insights were accurate and readable.
ActionWrote SQL to compute Recency/Frequency/Monetary quintile scores and segment labels, fixed a state-code geocoding bug via a DAX-mapped calculated column, and applied conditional-formatting to turn a sparse 5×5 R×F matrix into a readable heatmap.
ResultUncovered a 3.12% repeat-purchase rate (well below the ~20–30% e-commerce benchmark) and a strong Recency–Frequency correlation, reframing retention strategy around early recency decline as a churn signal.
